# Bound GWM to semantic outcome prediction

GWM will predict language-aligned visual outcomes, not physical dynamics, novel manipulation skills, feasibility, control, or safety. This scope preserves the paper's semantic-planning contribution and prevents capabilities supplied by other modules from being claimed as properties of GWM.

## Consequences

Claims about GWM generalization are limited to semantic visual-outcome prediction. The phase-one experiment targets cross-domain transfer within the Franka embodiment and does not test or claim cross-embodiment generalization, physical dynamics, or manipulation-skill transfer. Generalization of the complete robot system must be attributed to the composition of GWM with the proposal and execution layers.
